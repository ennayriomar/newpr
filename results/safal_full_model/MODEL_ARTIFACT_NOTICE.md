# Fitted model artifact attribution

The fitted hazelnut normal-reference bank derives from MVTec AD, published by MVTec Software GmbH under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. See https://www.mvtec.com/research-teaching/datasets/mvtec-ad and https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/ . Respect its noncommercial and share-alike terms for dataset-derived artifacts.

The fitted pcb2 normal-reference bank derives from VisA (Amazon Science), CC BY 4.0. See https://github.com/amazon-science/spot-diff and https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Dataset images are not included. Paths/hashes identify the actual normal support and calibration images used.

The external real DINOv2 ViT-B/14 pretrained checkpoint is separate. Pinned DINOv2, SubspaceAD, AnomalyDINO and official AD2 utility source snapshots retain their upstream license files and notices. No VisionAD repository source is copied or bundled; its paper inspires an independent cosine-search branch.

Few-shot support banks and source proxy error-estimator coefficients are measured fitted examples, not a universally trained industrial detector or published-method reproduction.
